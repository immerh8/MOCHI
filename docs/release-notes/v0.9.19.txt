Pre-release of MOCHI 1.0. Opening an archive that takes a while to read (on a network drive, or a very large one) now shows "Opening <name>…" over the window, and the window's buttons and menus wait until it is open, instead of looking as if nothing happened. An archive on a slow network drive no longer holds up other windows or other archives you open meanwhile, and "Extract to…" no longer waits on recent folders that sit on a drive that is asleep or gone. Settings, Extraction chooses where "Extract to…" starts: the Desktop (the default), the folder used last, or a folder you choose. "Add to archive", "Extract here" and "Extract to a new folder" from the right-click menu each get a small window of their own and run side by side, so a second archive can be made while the first is still being compressed; closing one window stops only its own job. Settings is now one section at a time (General, Extraction, Performance, Updates, Crash reports), each setting on one line with its switch, and the longer explanations behind the small "i". Archives made by earlier versions open as before, and the mochi command line is unchanged.
