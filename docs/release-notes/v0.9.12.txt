Pre-release of MOCHI 1.0. A new progress card while MOCHI creates, adds to, writes or extracts an archive: it shows how much is done ("56% complete", "296.6 MiB of 528.9 MiB"), about how long is left and how fast it is going, and has Pause, Resume and Cancel buttons. A paused job stops at its next safe point (within one 8 MiB piece) and goes on from there when resumed; Cancel still works while paused and leaves the archive as it was. If a job stops with an error, the card turns red, says what went wrong and offers Try again (not for an encrypted archive, whose passphrase is never kept). The small window of the Explorer verbs (Extract here, Add to archive) has the same card.
