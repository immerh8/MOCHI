Pre-release of MOCHI 1.0. Folders holding more than 2 GiB can now be archived: files are read into the archive one chunk at a time instead of being held in memory, so "Failed (LIMIT_EXCEEDED): The input holds more than 2147483648 content bytes" no longer appears (archiving 2.4 GB took about 40 MB of memory in our test). If a file changes size while it is being added to a TAR-compatible archive, nothing is committed and you are asked to try again. In the archive window, Back, Forward and Up are faded when they cannot be used, and Close is at the far right of the path bar.
