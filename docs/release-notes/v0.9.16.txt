Pre-release of MOCHI 1.0. More compression choices when you create an archive. For .mochi and .tar.zst there are now seven, from fastest to smallest file: Turbo, Very fast, Fast, Balanced (the default, unchanged), Maximum (formerly "Smallest"), Ultra and Extreme. Extreme is the slowest and uses the most memory; on .mochi archives Ultra and Extreme usually save little over Maximum, and they help most with a large .tar.zst. ZIP and .tar.gz offer the three levels their compression actually has: Fast, Balanced and Maximum. Changing the format moves a choice the new format lacks to the nearest one it offers. Archives made by earlier versions open as before, and the mochi command line is unchanged.
