Pre-release of MOCHI 1.0. The app is now in English, Thai and Simplified Chinese (Settings > Language; it follows the Windows language by default). Hold the left mouse button on empty space and drag to select several files, as in Explorer. New "Extract to Desktop" button and right-click entry: extracts into a new folder on the Desktop and never overwrites. Actions that cannot be used for the open archive (for example adding to a ZIP, 7z or RAR) are now greyed out, with the reason in the tooltip. The Test result now starts with a plain verdict; checks that do not apply to the archive are folded into Details. The developer self-test is gone from the app. The Explorer entries "Add to archive..." and Add to "<name>.mochi" show the MOCHI icon. MOCHI is now licensed under MIT only.
