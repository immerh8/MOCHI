Pre-release of MOCHI 1.0. Each window now has one title bar instead of two: the archive name, the main actions (Open, Add, Extract to..., Test, Find in archives, search) and MOCHI's own minimize, maximize and close buttons sit on a single row. New archive, Extract, Remove, Info and Help moved into the More menu and keep their shortcuts (Ctrl+N, Ctrl+E, Delete, Alt+Enter, F1). Drag the empty part of the bar to move the window; double-click it to maximize or restore. New on the command line: mochi find PATTERN FOLDER... lists the file names inside every .mochi archive under those folders, as Find in archives does in the app. It reads only names, writes nothing, and lists every archive it could not search (for example an Encrypted one, which Find cannot open without the passphrase); exit code 2 means the search was incomplete.
