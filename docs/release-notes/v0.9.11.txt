Pre-release of MOCHI 1.0. Compressing large files and folders is faster: MOCHI now compresses on every processor core (at most 8) instead of one, so a 512 MB file that took 2.3 s takes 0.7 s on a 4-core computer, and the archive it writes is exactly the same as before. To keep the computer more responsive during a long job, turn off Settings > Speed > "Compress on every processor core"; the choice is remembered. Adding many small files from disk is also faster (10,000 files of 2 KB: about 11 s before, under 2 s now).
